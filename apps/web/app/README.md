# App screens (overview)

One mobile-first web app, installable on the phone, with three areas. The source is private.

**Staff:** sign in · home · clock in and out (selfie and location) · attendance history ·
leave · medical claims · requests · profile with staff ID card · privacy notice

**HR:** live dashboard · alerts · approvals (leave, claims, time fixes, home locations) · employees and
documents · attendance with adjustments and export · offices, sites and rules · company settings

**Vendor console:** companies and their documents · charts · feature switches · releases and
rollback · operations dashboard with maintenance mode and emergency stop
